Initial release.

- Playback controls: play, pause, stop, next, prev
- Now playing display with track info and position
- Library search with interactive selection (fledge-v1 protocol)
- Playlist browsing with interactive selection
- Volume control (up/down/absolute)
- Dual command aliases: `apple-music` and `am`
